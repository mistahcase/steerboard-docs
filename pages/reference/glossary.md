# Glossary

**Account.** What your company has with Steerboard: its people, groups and
projects. Nothing crosses from one account to another.

**Account manager.** Someone who runs the account: invites people, makes
groups and changes the account's settings.

**Action, activity.** Work that takes time, drawn as a boat.

**Agreed dates.** An item's dates as agreed with the client. Delays are
measured against them. In the details panel: **Agreed dates**.

**Draft.** Work whose dates aren't agreed yet. Drawn with a dashed outline;
never late.

**Delayed.** Agreed work now planned to end later than agreed, but not due yet.
Amber.

**Group.** A firm or team on your engagements: your
consultancy, a client, a vendor. People belong to groups; projects' teams are
picked from them.

**Lane.** When items on one row overlap in time, one goes down a lane, so
nothing is hidden.

**Lead.** The person who leads a workstream, or owns a work package.

**Link.** "B can't start until A is finished." A dotted line with an arrow.

**Milestone.** A moment, such as a sign-off. A diamond.

**Overdue.** Agreed work past its agreed end and not done. Red.

**Owner (of work).** The person who does it.

**Owner, editor, viewer.** What someone can do on a project: everything, change
the plan, or only look.

**Period.** What a status report covers: since the last report was issued, and
the next one ahead.

**Row.** A line on the board, holding as many items as fit side by side.

**Work package.** A bracket over some work, rolling up its dates, progress
and status. Put work in one from the right-click menu on the plan.

**Working day.** Monday to Friday, less the project's holidays. Lengths are
counted in working days.

**Workstream.** A section of the plan, lined off with its name at the left.
