# The marks

Steerboard draws with the plan slide's own vocabulary. Colour only ever means
how the work stands: navy and black draw the work; green, amber and red mean
done or on track, at risk, and late; the bright blue means "you're touching
this".

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| A navy boat: square at the start, pointed at the end | An activity with agreed dates. |
| A white boat with a dashed outline | A draft: pencilled in, dates not agreed yet. |
| Green filling a boat from its start | What's done of it. |
| A green boat with a check before its name | Completed. It ends on the day it was finished. |
| A boat going on in light blue past its agreed end, outlined amber | Delayed: planned to end later than agreed, not due yet. |
| The light blue part outlined red | Overdue: past its agreed end and not done. It grows to today until it's done. |
| A thin grey bar with end ticks under a boat | Its agreed dates, left behind when the work moved. |
| A black diamond | A milestone. |
| A green diamond with a check | A completed milestone. |
| A hollow diamond | Where a moved milestone was agreed. |
| A black bar with short hanging ends, a name beneath | A work package, over the work inside it. Dashed while it's a draft. |
| A fine dotted line with an arrow | A link: the next item waits for this one. |
| A dashed black line down the board | Today. |
| A grey band down the board | A holiday. |
| A small coloured dot and a few words | A status, as on a status slide: "On track", "+3 days", "At risk", "Late". |
| A blue ring, round white handles, a blue dot at an item's end | Selected. Drag the ends to change dates, the dot to link. |
