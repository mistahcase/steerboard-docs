# What's new

What has changed in Steerboard, newest first.

## November 2026

**Start from a template, or from last year's project.** **New project** now
asks what to start from: a blank plan, one of four templates (a consulting
engagement, a system roll-out, a sourcing project, a programme), or a copy of
one of your own projects with everything a draft again. See
[Your first project](getting-started/first-project.md#start-a-project).

**Find, and a fuller filter.** Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> on the plan,
type part of a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go to it. **Filter** now
also shows one person's work, or only what's overdue, slipped, a draft or
done; the rest fades where it is. See
[Find and filter](plan/board.md#find-and-filter).

**Who owns what, on the plan.** Choose **Show** in the plan's toolbar and tick
**Owners**: each item's owner follows its name as initials, and the report's
plan slide does the same. See [names](plan/items.md#names).

**A year or two on one screen.** The time scale has a fourth setting,
**Quarters**. And **Fit**, beside it, picks the scale and the size at which
the whole plan shows. The size slider is now under **Show**. See
[The board](plan/board.md#fit-and-size).

**A key to the marks.** Tick **Key** under **Show** for a small list of the
marks your plan is drawn with and what each is called. See
[The marks](reference/marks.md).

**One set of words for where work stands.** It's **Draft**, **Planned**,
**In progress** and **Done** everywhere: on the cards, in the details panel
and in the menus. The cards' first and third columns were called Identified
and Started.

**Help in one place.** The question mark at the top is now a menu: the docs
for the screen you're on, the keyboard shortcuts (press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere),
this page, and feedback. A new, empty plan shows three first steps at the
bottom left of the board.

**Smaller things.** The button for a project's settings says **Settings**.
The **Today** button keeps its name on a laptop. Rows that aren't in a
workstream are named **General** on the plan, as they are in the report.
