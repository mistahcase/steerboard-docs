# What's new

What has changed in Steerboard, newest first.

## November 2026

**Your words in the report.** Under the status you can now write **Key
messages**, **Decisions needed** and **Risks**, a line each. They go on the
slides right after the status summary. See
[Your words](report/index.md#your-words).

**Issued reports are kept.** Each report you issue is kept as it was. **Past
reports**, on the Report screen, lists them with how the status and the finish
have moved, and opens any of them to read, present and download again. See
[Past reports](report/index.md#past-reports).

**What a milestone depends on.** In a milestone's details, **What this depends
on** lights up the linked work leading to it and says which steps have no
room to spare. See [Links between work](plan/links.md#what-a-milestone-depends-on).

**My work.** A new page, beside **Projects**, lists what's yours across every
project: overdue, in progress, starting this week. Start work, move its
progress, finish it and write an update, from your phone. Contributors land
there when they sign in. See [My work](my-work.md).

**Projects as a table.** On **Projects**, choose **Table** to see every
project as a line you can sort, and **Only late or at risk** to see the ones
that need you. A project's status there is now how it stands today. See
[Your first project](getting-started/first-project.md#find-archive-and-delete-projects).

**For you.** The bell in the bar at the top lists what Steerboard has to tell
you: work given to you, updates that name you, your work that's overdue, and
reports that are due. It works whether or not a project sends email. See
[A tour of the screens](getting-started/tour.md#the-bar-at-the-top).

**Updates on a piece of work.** Under an item's notes you can now write dated
updates: where it stands and why. The latest one goes beside the item in the
status report when it's overdue or delayed. See
[Updates](plan/details.md#updates).

**What changed since the last report.** The button with two arrows on the
plan lists what moved, was added, finished or agreed since the last report, or
since a day you pick, with who changed it. **Show on the plan** draws where
each moved item was. See [What changed](plan/changes.md).

**A list to type and paste into.** **List**, the new tab beside **Plan**, shows
the plan as a line for each item: type names, dates and owners straight in,
press Enter for the next line, sort by any column, and paste rows copied from
Excel. See [List](list.md).

**A link to the report, without signing in.** A project's owner can turn on a
view-only link for people who only need to read the status report. No account
needed, and you can give it a last day and a passcode. See
[Sharing a project](sharing/sharing.md#a-link-that-opens-without-signing-in).

**Issue and send.** When you issue a report you can tick who gets it by
email: how the plan stands, your key messages and a link to that report. See
[Issue the report](report/index.md#issue-the-report).

**A PDF, and the plan as a picture.** The arrow beside **Download .pptx** now
also saves the slides as a PDF, and the plan as a picture to download or to
copy and paste into an email. See
[Present and download](report/present.md#a-pdf-and-the-plan-as-a-picture).

**Start from a template, or from last year's project.** **New project** now
asks what to start from: a blank plan, one of four templates (a consulting
engagement, a system roll-out, a sourcing project, a programme), or a copy of
one of your own projects with everything a draft again. See
[Your first project](getting-started/first-project.md#start-a-project).

**Find, and a fuller filter.** Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd> on the plan,
type part of a name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to go to it. **Filter** now
also shows one person's work, or only what's overdue, delayed, a draft or
done; the rest fades where it is. See
[Find and filter](plan/board.md#find-and-filter).

**Who owns what, on the plan.** Choose **Show** in the plan's toolbar and tick
**Owners**: each item's owner follows its name as initials, and the report's
plan slide does the same. See [names](plan/items.md#names).

**A year or two on one screen.** The time scale has a fourth setting,
**Quarters**. And **Fit**, beside it, picks the scale and the size at which
the whole plan shows. The size slider is now under **Show**. See
[The board](plan/board.md#fit-and-size).

**A key to the marks.** Tick **Key** under **Show** for a small list of the
marks your plan is drawn with and what each is called. See
[The marks](reference/marks.md).

**One set of words for where work stands.** It's **Draft**, **Planned**,
**In progress** and **Done** everywhere: on the cards, in the details panel
and in the menus. The cards' first and third columns were called Identified
and Started. A piece of work is an activity everywhere (**New activity** said
action), and work planned later than agreed is **Delayed** everywhere (the
report said slipped).

**Help in one place.** The question mark at the top is now a menu: the docs
for the screen you're on, the keyboard shortcuts (press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere),
this page, and feedback. A new, empty plan shows three first steps at the
bottom left of the board.

**Smaller things.** The button for a project's settings says **Settings**.
The **Today** button keeps its name on a laptop. Rows that aren't in a
workstream are named **General** on the plan, as they are in the report.
