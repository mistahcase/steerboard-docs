# Workstreams and work packages

A plan has two ways of organising work, and they do different jobs.

- Workstreams are sections of the board, lined off as on a think-cell
  plan: a column of names down the left and a thin black rule across where each
  one starts. Every row belongs to one.
- Work packages are brackets: a black bar over the work inside it, rolling up
  its dates, progress and status. A work package can sit anywhere in a
  workstream.

## Workstreams

Every plan has at least one. A new plan starts with **Workstream 1**.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add one | Right-click a row and choose **Start a workstream here**: that row and the ones below it in its section become a workstream. Or choose **New workstream** under the names, or right-click free space. |
| Rename it | Click its name and choose **Rename**, or double-click the name. |
| Give it a lead | Click its name and choose **Set lead…**. The lead shows under the name, on the cards' lanes and in the report. |
| Move it | Drag its name up or down. A line shows where it goes; its rows and work move with it. Or use **Move up** and **Move down**. |
| Add a row to it | Click its name and choose **Add a row**. |
| Move a row into another | Right-click the row and choose **Move row to a workstream…**. |
| Remove it | Click its name and choose **Remove workstream**. Its rows and work stay, in the section above. A plan's last workstream stays. |

The dot beside a workstream's name says how its agreed work stands: green on
track, amber at risk, red late. It's empty while nothing in it is agreed yet.

### Show only some workstreams

The workstream filter in the toolbar says what it shows (**All workstreams**,
a name, or a number). Untick the ones to hide, or choose **Only** to show just
one. The choice holds on every screen, including the report and its slides,
and it's in the address, so a link shows the same. While it shows only some,
the filter button turns blue, so a narrowed view is never mistaken for the
whole plan.

## Work packages

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Make one | Select the items, right-click one and choose **Put in a new work package**. It works on a single item too. A bracket goes over them on a new row above, and you name it. |
| Unpack one | Right-click the bracket and choose **Unpack**. What was inside stays where it is. |
| Move an item in or out | Use **Work package** in the [details panel](details.md). |
| Move everything in a work package | Drag the bracket. |

A work package always spans what's inside it, from the earliest start to the latest
end; its dates follow as you move the work. Its progress is its work's,
weighted by working days, and its status can't be set by hand.

Which work package an item is in is separate from which row it's on: moving
an item to another row keeps its work package.
