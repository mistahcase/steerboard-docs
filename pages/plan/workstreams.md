# Workstreams and work packages

A plan has two ways of organising work, and they do different jobs.

- Workstreams are sections of the board, lined off as on a think-cell
  plan: a column of names down the left and a thin black rule across where each
  one starts. Every row belongs to one.
- Work packages are brackets: a black bar over the work inside it, rolling up
  its dates, progress and status. A work package can sit anywhere in a
  workstream, and can hold other work packages, as deep as you need.

## Workstreams

Every plan has at least one. A new plan starts with **Workstream 1**. Work
that is in no workstream has its own rows at the top, named **General**, as in
the report.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Add one | Right-click a row and choose **Start a workstream here**: that row and the ones below it in its section become a workstream. Or choose **New workstream** under the names, or right-click free space. |
| Rename it | Click its name and choose **Rename**, or double-click the name. A name starts with a capital letter. |
| Give it a lead | Click its name and choose **Set lead…**. The lead shows under the name, on the cards' lanes and in the report. |
| Move it | Drag its name up or down. A line shows where it goes; its rows and work move with it. Or use **Move up** and **Move down**. |
| Add a row to it | Click its name and choose **Add a row**. |
| Move a row into another | Right-click the row and choose **Move row to a workstream…**. |
| Remove it | Click its name and choose **Remove workstream**. Its rows and work stay, in the section above. A plan's last workstream stays. |

The dot beside a workstream's name says how its agreed work stands: green on
track, amber at risk, red late. It's empty while nothing in it is agreed yet.

### Show only some workstreams

The filter in the toolbar says what it shows: **Filter** on the plan and the
cards (**All workstreams** on the other screens), a name, or a number. Untick
the workstreams to hide, or choose **Only** to show just one. The choice holds on every screen, including the report and its slides,
and it's in the address, so a link shows the same. While it shows only some,
the filter button turns blue, so a narrowed view is never mistaken for the
whole plan. On the plan the same filter also shows one person's work, or work
that stands a certain way: see [Find and filter](board.md#find-and-filter).

## Work packages

Any activity can have sub-activities, and those can have their own. An
activity with something inside it is a work package.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Break an activity down | Right-click it and choose **Add a sub-activity**. The activity becomes a bracket, and the new activity inside it takes over its dates and progress, so the plan reads the same. Name it, then add more the same way. |
| Add an activity to a work package | Right-click the bracket and choose **Add a sub-activity**. |
| Put a bracket round items you have | Select them, right-click one and choose **Put in a new work package**. It works on a single item too. A bracket goes over them on a new row above, and you name it. |
| Put an item in a work package | Drag it into the box under the package's bracket. Or drag it over the bracket and hold it there for a moment, or select it and press <kbd>Tab</kbd>: it goes inside what's above it on the board. |
| Take an item out | Drag it onto a row outside the box: above the bracket, or below the package's last row. Or select it and press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>, or right-click it and choose **Take out one level**. |
| Collapse a work package | Choose the small arrow before its bracket, or right-click the bracket and choose **Collapse**. It's shown as one bar and what's inside it leaves the board. The arrow before the bar, or **Expand**, opens it again. |
| Unpack one | Right-click the bracket and choose **Unpack**. What was inside stays where it is, one level up. |
| Move everything in a work package | Drag the bracket. |

You can also pick an item's work package under **Work package** in the
[details panel](details.md).

### What's in a work package

A faint box under the bracket shows what a work package holds. It covers the
package's rows, down to the last row with anything inside it, and it's as wide
as the bracket. The box is the edge of the package:

- Something you add or drop inside the box is in the package. On a new row
  right after the last one, it's in the package if you drop it just above the
  line, and outside if you drop it below.
- Beside the box is outside, even on one of the package's rows. So is a new
  row at the bottom of the board.
- What's already in the package stays in it for as long as it's on the
  package's rows. Move it in time, however far, and the bracket and the box
  stretch or close up to follow.
- While you drag, the tip says when letting go would put the item in a work
  package or take it out of one.

To add to a work package past the end of its bracket, right-click the bracket
and choose **Add a sub-activity**, or add the item anywhere and drag it into
the box.

A work package always spans what's inside it, from the earliest start to the latest
end; its dates follow as you move the work. Its progress is its work's,
weighted by working days, and its status can't be set by hand. That holds at
every level.

Putting something inside an activity that has progress or agreed dates of its
own replaces them with what's inside it from then on. Steerboard says so when
it happens, and **Undo** takes it back.

A collapsed work package is collapsed for everyone, and on the plan slide and
in presentation mode too. The cards, the workload and the report's tables
still list the work inside it.

A work package's own bracket can sit on any row. Its box starts at the
bracket, so keep what it holds on the rows under it; **Arrange rows by work package**
(right-click free space) puts a plan back in that shape.
