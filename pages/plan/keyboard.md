# Keyboard

Everything you can drag on the board you can also do from the keyboard.

## The board

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Reach the board. |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Go from item to item in reading order: rows top to bottom, each left to right. |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Move the focused item a day. With [several selected](items.md#select-several), all of them. |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Change an activity's end. |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Put it on the row or lane above or below (a new row past the first or last). It joins or leaves a [work package](workstreams.md#whats-in-a-work-package) as it goes into or out of the package's box. |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Select the focused item. |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> on a selected item | Put it inside what's above it on the board, one level deeper. See [work packages](workstreams.md#work-packages). |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> on a selected item | Take it out one level. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Rename it. |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete it. With several selected, all of them. |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Cancel a drag, deselect, or close the details panel. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Redo. |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> | Zoom in, out, or back to 100%. |

## Screens

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>4</kbd> | Plan, Cards, Workload, Report. |

## Cards

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move between cards. |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Move a card to the previous or next stage. |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move a card to the lane above or below. |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open its details. |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete it. |

## Presenting

| Keys | What they do |
|---|---|
| <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>Page Down</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> | Next slide. |
| <kbd>←</kbd>, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> | Previous slide. |
| <kbd>Escape</kbd> | Leave the presentation. |

On a Mac, use <kbd>Cmd</kbd> where this page says <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.
