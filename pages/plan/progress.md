# Progress

An activity is **Not started**, **In progress** (with a percentage) or
**Completed**. A milestone is completed or not.

## Set it

- Drag the percentage tab that appears under an activity when you point at
  it (‹ 40% ›) to the left or right.
- Click the check beside a milestone.
- Right-click an item and pick its status.
- Or use **Status** and the progress slider in the [details panel](details.md).

On the [cards](../cards.md), drag a card to **Started** or **Done**, or slide the
progress bar on a started card.

## How it's drawn

What's done fills in green from the start of the boat, evenly along it as it's
drawn: 50% is halfway. Completed work is green all over, with a check before
its name, and ends on the day it was finished.

A completed milestone is a green diamond with a check.

## Changing the length keeps what's done

Making an activity that's in progress longer or shorter keeps the work already
done on the same days, so the percentage changes instead. 80% of a 10-day
activity stretched to 15 days becomes 53%.

## Correcting a completion day

When something is marked completed, Steerboard notes the day. If it actually
finished another day, change **Completed on** in the details panel, or drag
the end of the finished boat. The correction is kept in its history.

## Groups and the whole plan

A group's progress is its work's, weighted by working days. The toolbar shows
how much of the whole plan is done.
